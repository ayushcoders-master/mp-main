# NCRB data

`ncrb2022.json` is generated by `scripts/extract_ncrb.py` from the NCRB report *Crime in India 2022* (Volume I tables 1A.1, 1A.4, 1B.1, 1B.4) and the NCRB metro CSVs for crimes against women and cyber crimes.

All rates are per lakh (100,000) population. Population: Census 2011 for cities, mid-year 2022 projection for States/UTs.
Source: National Crime Records Bureau, Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India - https://ncrb.gov.in
